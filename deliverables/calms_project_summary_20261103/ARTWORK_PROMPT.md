# CALMS architecture artwork provenance

The architecture artwork was produced with the built-in image-generation tool and refined through targeted visual edits. The final raster contains no generated prose; every reader-facing label is added deterministically by `tasks/render_calms_project_summary.py`.

## Final edit prompt

Preserve a wide scientific workflow with one LC-MS/MS input panel, three parallel evidence panels, one blank CALMS integration panel and one ranked annotation-output panel. Remove all charts and all gray header bands. Use a pure white background, uniform very pale academic-blue panel fills, navy line art and thin blue arrows. Reserve the upper 22–25% of every panel as empty title space without adding a separate header strip. Use self-explanatory icons: three perturbed spectra converging to a stable embedding; diagnostic peaks connected to fragments and selected versus rejected candidates; biological sample vials connected to a metabolite network; and an ordered 1/2/3 candidate output. Keep the CALMS panel completely blank. Include no captions, headings, legends, pseudo-text, watermarks, gradients, shadows or 3D effects.

# LCNEC biology novelty audit (2026-09-01)

## Scope and rule

This audit asks what part of the current biology is genuinely incremental over prior LCNEC and lung-
neuroendocrine literature. Exact-phrase and concept searches were run for LCNEC with ADP-ribose/PARP,
quinolinate/QPRT and ascorbate/redox. Failure to retrieve a direct paper is not proof of absence; all
novelty language must therefore remain scoped to the searched literature and to the source HSST3n table.

## 1. ADP-ribose/PARP

PARP biology in high-grade lung neuroendocrine tumors is not new. Byers et al. reported high PARP1
expression in SCLC and LCNEC and preclinical PARP-inhibitor sensitivity (Cancer Discovery 2012,
doi:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-12-0112). The present paper must not claim discovery of PARP elevation or PARP
as a new LCNEC target.

The incremental observation is narrower: an author-unreported ADP-ribose connectivity-family signal
increased in 31/34 paired LCNEC tissues, and a separately frozen analysis found PARP1 and PARP2 increased
in an independent pure-LCNEC protein cohort. This is a metabolite-to-protein mechanism bridge that
prioritizes ADP-ribose/PARP turnover for follow-up. It does not identify the ADP-ribose isomer, prove
PARP-derived metabolite production or establish drug sensitivity.

## 2. Quinolinate/QPRT

Quinolinic acid is not a chemically novel cancer metabolite. Circulating quinolinic acid has been
associated with future lung-cancer risk in a 5,364-pair consortium study (Int J Cancer 2020,
doi:10.1002/ijc.32555), and QPRT-dependent use of quinolinate for NAD synthesis has been demonstrated in
glioma (Cancer Research 2013, doi:10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-12-3831). These studies establish biological
plausibility, not LCNEC-tissue replication.

The searched literature did not retrieve a direct paired-LCNEC tissue report linking a quinolinate
increase to lower QPRT. Our incremental result is therefore the specific Level-2 LCNEC tissue hypothesis
and its independent QPRT/NAD-protein context. Because IDO1, KYNU and HAAO also decreased while NMNAT3
increased, the correct novelty statement is a testable quinolinate-utilization-bottleneck or NAD-
redistribution model—not demonstrated de-novo-NAD flux.

## 3. Ascorbate/antioxidant remodeling

Oxidative-stress and pentose-phosphate biology in LCNEC is already reported in proteogenomic studies.
The current work cannot claim discovery of oxidative stress, PPP remodeling or antioxidant dependence.
Moreover, the independent pure-LCNEC panel was directionally mixed: GSR/G6PD/TKT/TALDO1 decreased while
TXNRD1 increased, and ascorbate transporters were not quantified.

The incremental observation is the recovery of an author-unreported, large-effect ascorbate Level-2
hypothesis within a measured source-reproduced antioxidant-pool pattern (GSH/GSSG up, ophthalmate down).
This is an abundance-remodeling result and a high-yield standard target, not evidence for uniform PPP
activation, vitamin-C dependence or redox flux.

## 4. ADP connectivity family

ADP is a biochemical currency hub and the library evidence maps to more than one full InChIKey. It has
strong paired abundance and a source-reproduced phosphorylated-nucleotide context, but no frozen
independent protein bridge specific to ADP. It should be presented only as a family-level nucleotide-pool
sentinel, not as a novel metabolite, energy-charge measurement or pathway-specific network discovery.

## Defensible novelty hierarchy

1. **Strongest mechanism bridge:** ADP-ribose-family abundance plus independent PARP1/2 context.
2. **Most discriminating chemical validation target:** quinolinate plus independent QPRT/NAD redistribution.
3. **Largest analytical effect:** ascorbate within an antioxidant-pool pattern, with mixed protein context.
4. **Supporting sentinel:** ADP connectivity family in phosphorylated-nucleotide accumulation.

The manuscript's methodological novelty is not any one known pathway. It is the evidence-calibrated
recovery of source-table-absent LCNEC signals, followed by explicit separation of spectral identity,
paired abundance, network context, independent protein support and standard-confirmation requirements.

# LCNEC external novelty update (2026-09-01)

## Search question

Does the current biology merely restate known LCNEC metabolism, or does it add a bounded result that
is not already supplied by the source atlas, known LCNEC genomics or known neuroendocrine PARP biology?

## Findings that are not novel by themselves

1. The 2026 source atlas already reports 1,052 metabolites and broad amino-acid, nucleotide and lipid
   remodeling in 34 paired LCNEC tissues. A second generic “LCNEC metabolic atlas” claim is therefore
   unavailable. Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopha.2026.119327
2. RB1-altered and STK11/KEAP1-altered LCNEC groups are established molecular classes. The external
   transcriptomic audit uses these groups as an expression-independent validation design; it does not
   claim to discover the classes. Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4995776/ and
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5849599/
3. High PARP1 expression in pulmonary high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma, including LCNEC, is already
   documented. PARP1 or PARP2 elevation alone is not a novel mechanism claim. Sources:
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3567922/ and
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11161257/
4. QPRT-mediated conversion of quinolinate into the de-novo NAD pathway is established biochemistry.
   The pathway connection cannot be presented as newly discovered chemistry. Source:
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5319230/

## Bounded incremental result

The defensible increment is the combination of four elements that the source article did not report:

- phenotype-blind recovery of an author-table-absent ADP-ribose connectivity-family signal and a
  Level-2 quinolinate hypothesis from qualified raw HSST3n features;
- strong paired tumor-to-adjacent abundance effects in the 34-patient discovery cohort;
- a fixed independent protein audit showing PARP1/PARP2 elevation and a mixed
  QPRT/HAAO/IDO1/KYNU/NADSYN1-down, NMNAT3-up NAD context; and
- an external 66-tumor LCNEC expression audit in which the same frozen axes align with clean,
  expression-independent genomic strata.

The external result is not uniformly pathway-wide: leave-one-gene-out analysis was fully robust for
redox (8/8 omissions), but the NAD and ADP-ribose contrasts were materially anchored by NMNAT3 and
PARP1, respectively. This dependence is part of the result, not a reason to relabel it as a broad flux
program.

This is best described as **algorithm-enabled recovery and cross-omics contextualization of previously
unreported LCNEC metabolite hypotheses**, not discovery of PARP biology, the kynurenine pathway, LCNEC
genomic subtypes or chemically novel metabolites.

## Strongest current biological proposition

The quinolinate result is the most discriminating chemical proposition: local quinolinate-like abundance
increases while the independent pure-LCNEC protein cohort shows lower QPRT and several upstream de-novo
NAD proteins, and the external genomic contrast shows strong NMNAT1/NMNAT3 heterogeneity. This supports
a testable quinolinate-utilization/NAD-redistribution model. It does not distinguish increased
production from impaired utilization, does not prove QPRT causality and remains Level 2.

The ADP-ribose result is the strongest cross-omics context proposition: the local family-level signal
increases while independent PARP1/PARP2 proteins increase, and external PARP1 expression differs by
genomic stratum. Because PARP biology is known and the metabolite remains a connectivity family, the
increment is the metabolite-family bridge, not PARP itself.

## Required wording

Allowed:

> The workflow recovered author-table-absent ADP-ribose-family and quinolinate hypotheses whose paired
> abundance patterns align with independently tested LCNEC protein and genomic-stratum contexts.

Forbidden:

- “four newly identified exact metabolites”;
- “PARP metabolism was discovered in LCNEC”;
- “quinolinate flux increased”;
- “QPRT loss causes quinolinate accumulation”;
- “the metabolite directions were replicated in the external transcriptomic cohort”; or
- “the three omics panels measure one common quantitative endpoint.”

## Remaining decisive validation

Same-method authentic-standard retention time and MS/MS remain the shortest route to a chemical upgrade,
with quinolinic acid first and ascorbic acid second. An independent LCNEC metabolomics cohort is required
for abundance replication. Stable-isotope tracing or perturbation is required for utilization, flux or
causal-enzyme claims.

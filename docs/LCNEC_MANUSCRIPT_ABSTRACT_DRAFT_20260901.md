# LCNEC manuscript abstract draft (bounded Level-2 claim)

## Working title

Evidence-calibrated recovery of dark metabolite hypotheses from paired LCNEC tissues using a mass-spectral foundation model

## Abstract

Most features in untargeted metabolomics remain unreported, but increased annotation coverage does not
necessarily imply increased correctness. We developed an evidence-calibrated workflow that combines
phenotype-blind analytical filtering, DreaMS spectral representations, frozen candidate ranking,
classical fragment evidence, paired-abundance analysis, reaction-network abstention and independent
proteomic context. We applied it to the public HSST3n raw-data arm of 34 paired pulmonary large-cell
neuroendocrine carcinoma (LCNEC) tumor and adjacent tissues. Of 1,138 precursor-retention-time families,
263 passed pooled-QC reproducibility, blank and dilution-response criteria; 42 overlapped the source
HSST3n table and 221 represented source-table-absent analytical headroom. On this shared denominator,
official DreaMS produced candidates for 158/263 families and the full multi-evidence workflow retained
66/263. Within 81 frozen abundance-selected dark modules, 12 hypotheses reproduced source-reported
cross-platform direction (12/12 concordant; Spearman rho=0.902), providing an orthogonal positive
control. Four author-unreported priorities were retained: ADP and ADP-ribose connectivity families, and
Level-2 ascorbate and quinolinate hypotheses. Their paired tumor-to-adjacent effects ranged from +1.556
to +5.407 log2, with 28-33 of 34 patients concordant. In a pre-registered independent LCNEC protein
panel, PARP1/2 increased, QPRT and several de-novo-NAD proteins decreased, and redox proteins showed a
mixed compensatory pattern, supporting distinct pathway contexts without confirming metabolite identity
or flux. In a second external cohort, all three frozen axes differed between 22 clean
STK11/KEAP1-altered and 17 clean RB1-altered LCNEC tumors under fixed multivariate, stage-sensitivity
and dispersion gates; this supplied expression-independent genomic-stratum context but not
tumor-versus-normal metabolite replication. A source-positive-control identity subset showed 17/19 structural concordance, while two
same-formula isomer errors survived the full confidence gate. Explicit rival auditing found same-formula
top-five candidates for three of the four priorities, and no pair of priority effects passed a frozen
patient-level covariation gate. These results establish a reproducible framework for algorithm-enabled
Level-2 hypothesis generation from metabolomic dark features while exposing the identity and mechanism
boundaries that require authentic standards and independent metabolite replication.

Here, author-unreported was verified against all 1,054 source-atlas identity rows by exact-name/structure
resolution and supported-adduct neutral-mass matching, rather than inferred only from HSST3n-table absence.

## One-sentence significance

A foundation-model annotation workflow can expand usable biological evidence beyond a published
metabolite table, but only when coverage, identity accuracy, pathway context and causal mechanism are
kept as separate, auditable claims.

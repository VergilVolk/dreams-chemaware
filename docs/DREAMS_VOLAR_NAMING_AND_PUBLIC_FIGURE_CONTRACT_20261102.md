# DreaMS-VOLAR public identity and figure contract

Date: 2026-11-02

## Public method identity

The reader-facing method name is **DreaMS-VOLAR**:

> **DreaMS-VOLAR: Vector, Orthogonal and Local Evidence with an Additive Residual for MS/MS Candidate Ranking**

The frozen implementation remains **Full23 v1.3.2** for provenance. `Full23`
must not be used as the main method name in the title, abstract, main figures or
public benchmark tables. The preferred figure label is **DreaMS-VOLAR (ours)**.

The name maps to the actual architecture:

- **Vector**: condition-robust and chemistry-refined spectral embeddings;
- **Orthogonal**: candidate-specific observed-versus-matched-null chemistry;
- **Local**: weighted entropy, peak-pair and learned local-fragmentation evidence;
- **Additive Residual**: a learned additive parent plus the query-centred full-
  embedding residual frozen in v1.3.2.

The exact name was not found in targeted OpenAlex or PubMed searches on
2026-11-02. This is a scientific-name collision screen, not a trademark opinion.

## Reader-facing figure hierarchy

The main benchmark must compare public methods, not mix internal components and
external competitors at the same visual level.

The public-method panel contains:

- DreaMS-VOLAR;
- weighted spectral entropy (WSE);
- official fine-tuned DreaMS;
- entropy and denoising search;
- public Spec2Vec;
- MS2DeepScore 2.x;
- MSBERT;
- SpecEmbedding.

Noise V1, Stage-1, Phase-A, FullMSG, P2b and ChemAware V2 remain visible in the
method diagram and evidence-family ablation, not as public competitors. RRF is
excluded because it is a retired negative result. BioAware is absent from this
closed-library ranking figure because no event-level sample context is present.

The figure suite is organized as follows:

1. a reader-facing algorithm schematic;
2. Recall@1 ladders, Top-k curves and DreaMS-style ROC curves;
3. precision-recall curves and paired query-level decision changes;
4. evidence-family strict-refit ablations and corrected/introduced exchanges;
5. a supplementary multi-metric rank profile.

Every figure is exported as 300-dpi PNG plus vector PDF and SVG. Plot source
tables, captions and a machine-readable manifest accompany the figures.

## Same-axis benchmark facts

The reader-facing GNPS common domain contains:

| Panel | Queries | Candidate molecules | Spectrum pairs | Near queries |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| identity-disjoint | 10,983 | 87,494 | 175,107 | 7,582 |
| formula-disjoint | 5,253 | 24,385 | 47,682 | 2,938 |

DreaMS-VOLAR Recall@1 is 88.400% and 89.644%, respectively. On the same axis it
exceeds official fine-tuned DreaMS by +3.014 and +2.779 percentage points, WSE by
+0.983 and +1.333 points, and MS2DeepScore 2.x by +9.242 and +8.110 points.
MS2DeepScore is explicitly marked as an auxiliary comparator because its native
objective is structural similarity rather than exact identity.

The main precision-recall panels report an AUPRC/AP advantage over WSE of +8.79
and +9.33 points. These candidate-level metrics complement, but do not replace,
query-level Recall@1 and Top-k retrieval metrics.

## Scientific claim boundary

GNPS and Enveda are consumed development resources. GNPS has also informed
method diagnosis and final implementation selection. Therefore these figures
support same-axis development comparisons and method characterization, not an
unopened external SOTA claim. No CDC or HUMAN score is shown until a frozen
evaluation is genuinely available.

The public figure package is generated by
`tasks/render_dreams_volar_publication_suite.py` and written to
`deliverables/dreams_volar_publication_figures_20261102/`.

## Visual precedents

The public benchmark adopts the useful conventions of the DreaMS paper: a
strongly highlighted proposed method, restrained grey background methods,
multi-panel task narration and metric-bearing curve legends. The method diagram
also follows the workflow-first principle used by MS2Query. These are visual
precedents, not evidence that the tasks or reported values are directly
interchangeable.

# LCNEC independent proteogenomic fixed-panel result

## Frozen design

The 22-protein panel, pure-LCNEC paired endpoint, two-sided Wilcoxon test, 22-test BH correction,
direction-stability rules, axis gate and missing-protein policy were frozen before the patient-level
protein matrix was opened. The deposited matrix contains 103 tumor/NAT pairs: 80 pure LCNEC and 23
combined LCNEC. Eighteen of 22 proteins were measured. TDO2, NMNAT2, SLC23A1 and SLC23A2 were missing
and were not replaced.

## Primary pure-LCNEC paired result

All three frozen axes pass the literal pre-registered axis gate, but only the ADP-ribose axis is
uniformly directional among its passing proteins. The other two axes must be described as remodeling,
not uniform activation or repression.

- **Quinolinate/de-novo-NAD context:** IDO1 (mean tumor−NAT −1.063 log2; q=1.15e−8), KYNU
  (−0.183; q=0.0603), HAAO (−1.103; q=2.38e−13), QPRT (−0.853; q=8.13e−10) and NADSYN1
  (−0.212; q=2.54e−4) pass downward, while NMNAT3 passes upward (+0.366; q=5.02e−5).
  The local quinolinate increase together with lower QPRT is compatible with reduced quinolinate
  utilization, but upstream enzymes also decrease and NMNAT3 changes oppositely. The defensible claim
  is NAD-pathway redistribution, not a proven linear flux bottleneck.
- **ADP-ribose turnover:** PARP1 (+1.319; q=1.79e−13) and PARP2 (+0.868; q=3.10e−12) pass upward with
  98.8% and 90.0% of patient pairs, respectively, in the same direction. This independently supports
  a PARP-associated ADP-ribosylation context for the local ADP-ribose-family accumulation. It does not
  identify the exact ADP-ribose isomer or prove its enzymatic source.
- **Ascorbate/redox context:** GSR (−0.252; q=5.71e−5), G6PD (−0.162; q=0.00284), TKT (−0.722;
  q=8.00e−13) and TALDO1 (−0.438; q=3.23e−10) pass downward, whereas TXNRD1 passes upward (+0.481;
  q=5.51e−8). Together with local ascorbate/GSH/GSSG accumulation and ophthalmate depletion, this is
  consistent with compartmentally mixed antioxidant-pool remodeling rather than uniform pentose-
  phosphate or glutathione activation.

## Secondary histology-context audit

The exploratory combined-versus-pure tumor comparison reproduces the independent article's reported
combined-LCNEC pentose-phosphate phenotype: G6PD (+0.402 median log2; q=3.995e−4), TKT (+0.719;
q=2.57e−4) and TALDO1 (+0.258; q=0.00189) are higher in combined tumors; PGD is directionally higher
with q=0.0513. This is a useful positive control for sample labels and clarifies why the pure-LCNEC
paired result must not be conflated with the combined subtype. PARP2 is lower in combined than pure
tumors (−0.740; q=0.00451), a secondary hypothesis only.

KEAP1 stratification is not evaluated because the deposit contains no author-curated binary label and
a naive non-synonymous call from the raw mutation table does not reproduce the article's reported
percentage.

## What this changes in the biology story

The strongest integrated mechanism is now an **ADP-ribose/PARP remodeling axis**, because the local
metabolite-family signal and independent paired PARP1/PARP2 protein signal are directionally coherent.
The quinolinate result supports a **NAD-pool redistribution** hypothesis centered on QPRT loss but does
not establish flux. The redox result supports **mixed antioxidant compensation** and explicitly rejects
a one-directional PPP narrative in pure LCNEC.

These are independent protein-context results, not independent metabolite replication. They do not
upgrade MSI Level-2/connectivity-family identities, replace authentic standards, establish enzyme
activity or flux, or support causal/therapeutic claims.

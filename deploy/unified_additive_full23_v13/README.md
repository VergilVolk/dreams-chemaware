# DreaMS Unified Additive Full23 v1.3

Full23 v1.3 is the formal development release selected on 2026-11-01. It preserves the Full23 v1 candidate-evidence score and adds the fixed bounded, query-centred tensor-set residual over the complete embeddings of Noise V1, ChemAware Stage-1, ChemAware Phase-A and Noise-ChemAware FullMSG. Reference count is not an input.

The release was selected because the query-balanced unique-query Top-1 endpoint increased; both constituent GNPS panels also improved. Corrected/introduced counts, confidence intervals and the historical lambda=2 risk statistic are reported but do not veto a positive primary ranking endpoint. This policy change is explicitly post-result and the bundle is not an untouched external-confirmation claim.

No upstream encoder or reranker was retrained for this release. The two checkpoints and exact runtime sources are under `assets/` and `runtime/`.
